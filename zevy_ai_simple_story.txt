Depending on what you need, different AI systems spring into action:

---

## 🧠 Meet Your AI Team

### Vyra AI: The Debate Expert
**When to use**: Complex questions that need deep analysis
**How it works**: 
- Searches Wikipedia, web, and news for latest info (and, when search is ON, can look at up to around 100 different websites about your topic).
- Two AI experts discuss your question from different angles using the same research.
- Combines their insights into one comprehensive answer
**Daily limit**: 25 questions

**Example**: Ask "Should we invest in renewable energy?" and get both economic and environmental perspectives with current data.

### Astra AI: The Research Genius  
**When to use**: Learning something new or researching topics
**How it works**:
- Automatically searches multiple knowledge sources (and, when search is ON, focuses on about 10 of the most useful websites plus Wikipedia and other open-data checks).
- Provides detailed, research-backed responses with a medium amount of detail so answers are not too short and not overwhelming.
- Perfect for homework, learning, or curiosity
**Daily limit**: 125 questions

**Example**: Ask "How does photosynthesis work?" and get a clear explanation with the latest scientific understanding.

### Groq Compound: The Knowledge Collector
**What it does**: Powers both Vyra and Astra by gathering information
**Sources**: Wikipedia + Web Search + News APIs
**Result**: You get answers that combine established knowledge with current events
